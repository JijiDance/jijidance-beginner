Questions

Good to know

Do the guests need dance experience?

Not at all. The class is designed for complete beginners, step by step, so every guest can join in and have fun.

Is it suitable for corporate team building for women?

Yes. It is a light, joyful and inclusive activity for women's teams, wellness days and ladies-only corporate events.

Where does the experience take place?

At your home, villa, hotel or venue in Abu Dhabi, Al Ain, Dubai and across the UAE, upon availability.

What do the guests need?

Just comfortable clothes and a smile. Dance belts are provided for every guest.

Would you like to keep dancing afterwards?

Many guests do! Jiji teaches ladies-only bellydance classes for beginners in Abu Dhabi, as well as private lessons. See the courses.
