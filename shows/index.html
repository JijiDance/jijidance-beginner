Questions

Good to know

How much does a private bellydance show cost in the UAE?

Private bellydance performances start from AED 1,500. Customized packages are available upon request, depending on the date, duration, location and the experience you have in mind.

Where do you perform?

Shows take place in Abu Dhabi, Al Ain and Dubai, across the UAE and in the Gulf countries, upon availability.

Do you perform at weddings and henna parties?

Yes. Weddings, henna nights and engagements are some of the most requested events, including ladies-only celebrations.

Is the show suitable for ladies-only events?

Absolutely. Ladies parties and ladies-only celebrations are welcome, with full privacy and discretion.

Can we choose the music?

Of course. Your favorite Arabic songs, or a special song for the bride or guest of honor, can be included in the performance.

How far in advance should we book?

Ideally two to four weeks ahead, especially for weekends and wedding season. For last-minute events, send a message and availability will be checked right away.
