Who I Am

A girl from Brazil who
moved to the desert…

"I left Brazil on International Dance Day, in 2013, to experience bellydance at its origin - and without knowing it, I stepped into the biggest adventure of my life!"

For over 13 years I've lived and worked across the Arab world, performed on prestigious stages, taught students of more than 20 nationalities, and opened my own dance school right here in Abu Dhabi.

I'm a mother, postgraduate in Exercise Physiology, bellydancer and instructor from more then a decade.

I believe every woman hold a great strength inside herself - and the dance is able to awaken it!
